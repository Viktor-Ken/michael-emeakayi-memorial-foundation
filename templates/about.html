About the Foundation

Honouring Legacy. Empowering Generations.

Remembering High Chief Michael Emeakayi

The Michael Emeakayi Memorial Foundation is a registered non-profit organisation established in loving memory of High Chief Michael Emeakayi, a distinguished farmer, philanthropist and community leader from Umuohi, Okija.

His life was shaped by compassion, generosity and a deep commitment to education, agriculture and service. Those who knew him described a man who gave freely, taught willingly, and worked tirelessly for the good of his people.

The Foundation was set up by his children in April 2025 so that the values he lived by would continue to make a difference long after his passing.

Our Vision

To build a self-reliant, educated and empowered society through integrated grassroots programmes in education, agriculture and digital skills.

Our Mission

To deliver transformative, community-led initiatives that promote academic excellence, agricultural innovation and digital empowerment for young people and families in underserved communities.

Who we are

The Michael Emeakayi Memorial Foundation serves as the umbrella body for a growing portfolio of developmental programmes. Founded in April 2025 by his children, the Foundation is dedicated to advancing local development in Umuohi and beyond, with a focus on youth, education and sustainable livelihoods.

We are a small organisation with a big mandate. Our programmes target the same areas that mattered most to the late Chief Michael: children who need educational support, young people who want practical farm skills, and a new generation learning to navigate the digital economy.

Where your support goes

Education

Primary and secondary school scholarships, book drives and mentorship for students across Umuohi and Okija.

Agriculture

Climate-smart farming training for young people, with harvests shared directly with families who need it most.

Digital Skills

Foundational IT training, launching in 2026, to help students compete in the modern economy.

Community

The Annual Harvest event, scholarships, farming awards and a volunteer network that connects Umuohi to supporters at home and abroad.
