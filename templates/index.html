Honouring Legacy. Empowering Generations.

The Michael Emeakayi Memorial Foundation is a registered non-profit founded in memory of High Chief Michael Emeakayi, a farmer, philanthropist and community leader. His work lives on through our scholarships, our farms, and our classrooms.

Get in touch Explore initiatives

Our mission

To deliver transformative, community-led initiatives that promote academic excellence, agricultural innovation and digital empowerment for young people and families in underserved communities.

What we do

Three programmes, one goal: to give young people in Umuohi and beyond real chances to get ahead.

S

Academic Scholarship Awards

Prizes that reward academic excellence among primary and secondary students, with a focus on STEM, business and agriculture.

Read more
A

Agricultural Empowerment Programme

Hands-on training in climate-smart farming, with annual awards and produce shared with vulnerable families.

Read more
IT

IT Skills Training

Digital literacy and foundational tech education, launching 2026 with a dedicated training centre.

Read more

The Annual Harvest

Each year we gather students, educators, families and supporters for a single celebration of education, agriculture and community impact. Scholarships are awarded, harvests are shared, and funds are raised for the year ahead.
