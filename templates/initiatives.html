Our work

Three core initiatives

Each programme honours something the late Chief stood for: strong learners, skilled farmers and a generation ready for the digital age.

01

Academic Scholarship Awards

We celebrate academic excellence among primary and secondary school students, placing a particular focus on STEM, business and agriculture subjects. Hard work in the classroom deserves recognition, and these awards give promising young people the encouragement to keep going.

  • Annual awards for primary and secondary students
  • Recognition across STEM, business and agricultural studies
  • Presented at the Annual Harvest and Fundraising Event
02

Agricultural Empowerment Programme

Young people are given hands-on training in climate-smart farming techniques that protect the soil and lift yields. Every year the Foundation recognises the best young farmers with awards, and part of the harvest is shared with vulnerable families in Umuohi.

  • Practical training in climate-smart farming methods
  • Annual awards for outstanding young farmers
  • Produce from the programme shared with vulnerable households
03

IT Skills Training Launching 2026

Digital literacy and foundational tech education will prepare students for the digital economy. The programme begins with the establishment of a dedicated IT training centre, giving young people in Umuohi and Okija a place to learn, practice and build real skills.

  • Community digital literacy classes
  • Foundational tech education for students
  • A dedicated IT training centre in the community

How it all comes together

Our programmes reach their peak once a year. At the Annual Harvest, Award Presentation and Fundraising Event, students collect their scholarships, young farmers are honoured, and supporters from Nigeria and the diaspora come together to fund the next cycle of work.
